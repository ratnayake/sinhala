## 1. Introduction and scope

This statement explains how Ratcon ("we", "us") handles personal data in connection with:

- **The EasyAkuru app**: a Windows 10 and Windows 11 system-tray app that lets you type Sinhala
  phonetically ("Singlish") in any application. For example, typing `mama` produces මම.
- **This website**: the pages under `https://ratnayake.info/easyakuru/`.
- **Distribution through the Microsoft Store**, to the extent described in section 7.3.

EasyAkuru is published by Ratcon and written by Isuru Ratnayake. We have tried to write this
statement in plain language and to describe only what the app and website actually do. It does
not cover other companies' products or websites, including the apps you type into while using
EasyAkuru, which have their own privacy practices.

## 2. Personal data we collect

We designed EasyAkuru so that we do not need to collect personal data at all.

### What we do not collect

- We do not receive your keystrokes, the text you type, or anything derived from it. The app has
  no network features and cannot send them anywhere.
- We do not collect usage statistics, telemetry, crash reports, device identifiers or location
  from the app.
- There are no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no in-app purchases.
- This website does not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, web fonts or third-party
  scripts.

### What is involved

- **Data kept on your device.** The app saves your settings and the word choices you make in its
  suggestion box to files in your Windows user profile. These files stay on your PC; we never
  receive them. See section 7.1.
- **Website request data.** When you visit this website, our hosting provider, Cloudflare,
  processes the technical information any web server needs to deliver a page, such as your IP
  address, browser user agent, the page requested and the time. See section 7.2.
- **Messages you send us.** If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you
  include it, and whatever you write in the message and any attachments.
- **Microsoft Store data.** If you get EasyAkuru from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft processes
  data about your purchase and installation under its own privacy statement. See section 7.3.

## 3. How we use personal data

- **On your device, the app** uses what you type only to show Sinhala suggestions and insert the
  Sinhala text, and uses its saved files only to remember your settings and put your preferred
  spellings first.
- **Emails you send us** are used only to reply to you, to investigate and fix the problem you
  report, and to improve EasyAkuru based on your feedback.
- **Website request data** is processed by Cloudflare to deliver the website and to protect it
  against abuse, such as denial-of-service attacks.

We do not use personal data for advertising, profiling or automated decision-making, and we do
not sell it.

## 4. Reasons we share personal data

The app has nothing to share: it never sends data off your PC. For the limited personal data we
do handle (mainly emails you send us), we share it only:

- with service providers that operate on our behalf, namely our website host (Cloudflare) and
  our email provider, only as needed to run those services;
- when you ask us to or agree to it;
- when we believe in good faith that the law requires it, for example to respond to a valid
  legal request, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the public or us.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data.

## 5. How to access and control your personal data

### Data the app keeps on your PC

Everything the app saves is in the folder `%AppData%\EasyAkuru`, which you control directly:

- **See it:** open the folder and view `settings.json` and `user-dictionary.json` in any text
  editor.
- **Delete your word choices:** choose **Exit** from the EasyAkuru tray menu, then delete
  `user-dictionary.json`. EasyAkuru starts again with no learned choices.
- **Delete everything:** exit EasyAkuru and delete the whole `EasyAkuru` folder. If you used an
  earlier version called "SinhalaInput", also delete `%AppData%\SinhalaInput` if it exists (see
  section 7.1).
- **Pause it:** press **Ctrl+Space**, or clear **Enabled** in the tray menu, to type ordinary
  text without EasyAkuru converting it.
- **Stop it starting at sign-in:** turn EasyAkuru off in Windows **Settings › Apps › Startup**,
  or on the **Startup apps** page of Task Manager. Versions not installed from the Microsoft
  Store also offer this option in EasyAkuru's Settings window.
- **Uninstall it:** go to Windows **Settings › Apps › Installed apps**, select EasyAkuru, and
  choose **Uninstall**. For a Microsoft Store installation, Windows normally stores the app's
  files in a location private to the app and removes them when you uninstall. If you installed
  EasyAkuru another way, or want to be sure, delete the folders listed above.

### Data we hold

The only personal data we hold about you is any email correspondence you have had with us. You
can ask us to access, correct, delete or export it, or object to how we use it, by emailing
**isuru.sampath@ratnayake.info**. We may need to confirm that the request comes from you, and we
will reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to
complain to your local data protection authority.

For website request data held by Cloudflare, and for Microsoft Store data held by Microsoft, see
the links in section 7.

## 7. Product-specific details

### 7.1 The EasyAkuru app

#### Keystrokes

To convert text in any application, EasyAkuru uses a standard Windows system-wide ("low-level")
keyboard hook, which lets it see each key you press. This is how a system-wide phonetic keyboard
has to work. EasyAkuru handles those keys as follows:

- The Latin letters of the word you are currently typing are held in memory only until the word
  is finished (converted to Sinhala or kept as Latin) or discarded, and are then cleared.
- Keystrokes are never written to disk, logged, or sent over a network. The app's source code
  includes an explicit rule that keystroke data must never be logged to any file, console or
  diagnostic output.
- While EasyAkuru is paused, it still receives keys from Windows but ignores them.

#### Password fields (best effort)

Before handling each key, EasyAkuru checks whether the focused box is a standard Windows password
box. If it is, EasyAkuru does not buffer, suggest, convert or learn from anything typed there,
discards any partly typed word, and lets your keys pass through unchanged. This check is **best
effort**: it recognizes password boxes built with the standard Windows edit control, but some
apps and websites draw their own password boxes, and EasyAkuru may not recognize those. Even
then, keystrokes are not stored or sent anywhere; the only effect is that the suggestion box
could appear. You can pause EasyAkuru with Ctrl+Space before typing a password if you prefer.

#### Files EasyAkuru saves

| File | What it contains | When it is written |
|---|---|---|
| `%AppData%\EasyAkuru\settings.json` | Two on/off preferences: whether conversion is enabled, and whether EasyAkuru starts when you sign in. | When you change a setting. |
| `%AppData%\EasyAkuru\user-dictionary.json` | Your word choices: for each word where you picked a suggestion by number, the Latin spelling you typed (for example `mama`) and the Sinhala spelling you picked. | When you pick a suggestion by number. Never from a recognized password box. |

These are ordinary files in your Windows user profile. EasyAkuru never uploads, syncs or shares
them. When upgrading from an earlier version called "SinhalaInput", EasyAkuru copies these two
files from `%AppData%\SinhalaInput` to `%AppData%\EasyAkuru` once, if the new files do not
already exist. It copies rather than moves them, so the old copies remain until you delete them.

#### Starting at sign-in

EasyAkuru starts automatically when you sign in to Windows. The Microsoft Store version uses the
Windows startup task feature for packaged apps; other installations use a per-user entry in the
Windows registry that contains only the path to the app. Neither involves sending any
information anywhere. See section 5 to turn it off.

#### No network access, telemetry or ads

EasyAkuru contains no networking code. It does not check for updates itself, collect usage
statistics or crash reports, or include any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking
libraries. Updates to the Microsoft Store version are delivered by the Microsoft Store.

## 8. How we secure data

The strongest protection is not collecting data in the first place: your keystrokes stay in
memory on your PC, and the app has no networking code. The files EasyAkuru saves are stored in
your Windows user profile and are protected by Windows' normal access controls for that profile;
EasyAkuru does not encrypt them itself, so anyone with access to your Windows account can read
them. You can use Windows features such as BitLocker device encryption to protect your whole
drive. This website is served only over HTTPS. We keep email correspondence in an
access-controlled email account.

## 9. Where we store and process data

Data the app saves stays on your PC. Website request data is processed by Cloudflare, which runs
a global network, so it may be handled in a data centre outside your country. Emails you send us
are stored by our email provider and may likewise be processed outside your country. Where the
law requires it, we rely on our providers' safeguards for international transfers.

## 10. How long we keep data

- **Keystrokes:** only in memory, until the current word is finished or discarded.
- **App files on your PC:** until you delete them or uninstall the app, as described in
  section 5.
- **Emails:** for as long as needed to handle your request and keep a reasonable record of
  support history, after which we delete them. You can ask us to delete them sooner.
- **Website request data:** kept by Cloudflare for the periods described in its privacy policy.

## 11. Children

Neither has accounts, and the app sends nothing off the device. If a child has emailed us and a
parent or guardian would like that message deleted, contact us and we will delete it.
